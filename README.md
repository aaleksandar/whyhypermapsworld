Playful (feels like Geocashing, you’re constantly exploring), visual style in palette of colors

Allows you to enter different worlds - of architecture, food, laptop-friendly cafes, vegan spots, affordable clothes, japanese hair salons, places to go with kids, etc. See map through the lens of your community (Future vision w/ AR Glasses - change frequency, see different worlds.)

As you enter different worlds, the map is designed in artistic way to represent these worlds

Imagine if Google Maps had a child w/ Reddit

The map helps you find your vibe.

See areas on the map like safety areas, hipster areas, family areas, hot/cool areas, shaded areas etc etc

## How it Works

So how we want to approach this is that the map basically has different layers of understanding of you and the world. So understanding the world is like factual data, like, you know, does a certain gym have a sauna or does a certain coffee shop have Wi-Fi?

Then there are more descriptive data, like, and subjective data, like, does this coffee shop have a good vibe or is the service in this place good or not? It's really more descriptive and based on preferences. Or is this Chinese food good or not?

Like a foreigner might, like a westerner might say it's good, but a Chinese person might say that it's not really good. So it's really contextual. If you wanna see people like you who have reviewed certain spaces. And then finally there is the vibe of like through your friends and connections, like where have my friends been?

How have they reviewed spaces? And that also gives us context of your social graph in maps. And as a visionary thing, we want to also understand spaces on a more neurological perspective, like, hey, how does your brain, like maybe you have an EEG wearable and maybe we can match you with spaces that are going to suit your nervous system.

So those are the data points we're using to basically approximate the vibe and match you with places of your vibe, almost like synchronicity. So you can at the right time be matched with the right place.

## Contribution incentives and mechanics

Another mechanics is that, like, it's fun to contribute to the map. People feel like they want to contribute to their world. For example, vegan spots, like, say, you know, it doesn't, maybe you go to this Chinese spot where you can boil your own food and you don't have to just choose meat.

So this is a great vegan spot, even though it's not officially vegan. And people want to contribute to their community and they want to, when they see a closed space, they want to update information because they will be rewarded in a much more fun way than Google Maps because they are a character, they are like a guide

And they're also part of the news world, not really releasing data to like who knows whom, but actually they're releasing data to the community for people like them who they want to help.

## Ask questions in your area

So multiple times I was somewhere and I really just wanted to ask the universe, like, hey, universe, give me affordable spots for food or give me really local dishes or give me where can I find art around me right now? And this search would really need me to go on like Google or like TikTok and like I wouldn't really know what's around me really.

So I just want to ask, like, I wish ideally that there was a local that just knows me and knows my vibe and tells me where to go. So this is another feature in our map. You're just gonna chat with the community. The community knows who you are, it knows where you are and it knows what you need.

And then similar to like Uber or Grab dynamics where a car can't pick you up, like we actually have an AI kind of saying what it thinks that can help you and answers your query, but also maybe one or two people in the area might answer your query too and give you some human-level insights of where you can go.

So this is a unique feature for us.

In other words: Baciš pitanje u etar: Ko sam, gde sam, šta tražim (needs, keywords). U realtime se matchuje sa ljudima koji su slični meni, na koje su se keyworde subscribovali. (Ili sam mayor of gyms, ili naš sistem zna pa ih je autosubscribeovao) Uber-like.

## See context behind pins

Another mechanics is that when you tap a pin, you want to see all the context behind that pin. For example, for gyms, you tap a gym and obviously you see the photos, but you see the photos that you care about, like using the equipment or the type of people who are there or something else.

And then you can see people discussing about different things, like community feeling or equipment quality or cleanness or air quality. Or maybe if you're a woman, you will see like how safe is the gym, can women feel safe there, et cetera, et cetera.

So, yeah, you tap a pin and you see all the things discussed about that specific location, and also you see like the highlights and what is that location really best for.

## Rewards / Gamification

If the place offers rewards, I want to see those rewards as I come into the space, and maybe they have certain standpoints. I want to be able to save those standpoints in my map. And as I earn rewards, I want to be able to redeem those rewards in spaces that accept them.

## UX

Another UX improvement, so when I look for food and I tap pins, it's came as a search result, I want to see photos of food. Or if I type cool ambiance and I tap a pin, I wanna see places like photos of the space.

I don't wanna see photos of food or people. So the map kind of knows what to show you at which point. Also, like the map shows you like these zones of the city, like the save zone, the tourist zone, the hipster zone, the new zone, industrial zone, like zones where you maybe need to, maybe are not accessible at a certain time, et cetera.

So the UX is really dynamic and it really shows you the true world around you.

Also, when I search for space on my mobile, I don't want to see closed spaces. I want to see open spaces by default. I want to see spaces that maybe have happy hours right now. I want to see spaces that have like events happening right now.

I want to see that there is a farmer's market around me, or that there's a... if I open like the world of, you know, tourism, I want to see that there's a tourist spot. I want to see the stories behind places.

When I enter a cafe, if I'm part of the Wi-Fi friendly world's map, I want this Wi-Fi to connect automatically to my phone.
